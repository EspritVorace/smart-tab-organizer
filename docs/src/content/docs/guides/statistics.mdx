The **Statistics** page centralizes the extension's activity in the active workspace. Counters are scoped per workspace; switching workspace immediately swaps the figures.

It is split into **four sub-pages** that you browse via the tab bar at the top of the page: **Summary**, **Rules**, **Sessions**, and **Storage**. Each sub-page has its own deep link (`#stats`, `#stats/rules`, `#stats/sessions`, `#stats/storage`) and the active sub-page appears in the header breadcrumb.

## Summary \{#summary}

The landing view gathers the headline indicators plus a look at the week.

<ThemeImage
  lightSrc={summaryLight}
  darkSrc={summaryDark}
  alt="Statistics Summary sub-page with its six headline KPIs and the This week block."
/>

Six headline tiles:

- **Groups Created**: cumulative total of Chrome groups created.
- **Tabs Deduplicated**: cumulative total of duplicates closed.
- **Local storage**: total size used in local storage.
- **Pinned**, **Active**, **Archived**: number of sessions per bucket.

Below, the **This week** block compares the current week's activity with the previous one, across two series (**Groupings** and **Deduplications**) each carrying a small trend badge.

## Rules

The detail of grouping and deduplication activity.

<ThemeImage
  lightSrc={rulesLight}
  darkSrc={rulesDark}
  alt="Statistics Rules sub-page with Totals, This week, and the most active rules ranking."
/>

### Totals

Two counters cumulated since first use:

- **Groups Created**: incremented every time a new Chrome group is created. Joining an existing group does not count.
- **Tabs Deduplicated**: incremented every time a duplicate is closed. An **Undo** via the notification button decrements the counter.

Below, two contextual indicators: the **number of active rules** and the **in use since** date.

### This week \{#this-week}

Compares the current week's activity with the previous week: **Groupings** (groups created) and **Deduplications** (duplicates closed). The delta (`+15 vs last week`, for example) shows the change against the prior week.

### Most active rules (30 days) \{#top-rules}

A ranking of rules by event volume (groupings + deduplications) over the past 30 days. Useful to spot the rules pulling their weight and the ones that have gone dormant and could be cleaned up.

### Reset the counters \{#reset}

The **Reset Stats** button at the bottom of the Rules sub-page zeroes every counter of the active workspace. A confirmation is requested. This action affects neither rules, nor sessions, nor settings: only the counters are wiped.

<Aside type="caution">
The reset is final. If you want to keep a record of the history before zeroing, export the workspace first with **Include statistics history** checked (see [Import / Export, workspaces](/guides/import-export#workspaces)).
</Aside>

## Sessions \{#sessions}

All the metrics tied to your sessions.

<ThemeImage
  lightSrc={sessionsLight}
  darkSrc={sessionsDark}
  alt="Statistics Sessions sub-page with volumes, activity, categories and domains, and the overview."
/>

### Session volumes

Sums up the current state of your sessions, **archives excluded** by default:

| Tile | Content |
|---|---|
| **Total** | Active + pinned sessions. |
| **Pinned** | Profiles shown in the popup and on the home page. |
| **Active** | Ordinary unpinned sessions. |
| **Archived** | Archived sessions (see [Sessions, archive](/guides/sessions#archive)). |
| **Saved tabs** | Sum of tabs contained in active and pinned sessions. |
| **Saved groups** | Sum of Chrome groups contained in the sessions. |

Under the tiles, three qualitative indicators: average tabs per session, the largest session, and the percentage of sessions with a category and the percentage with a note.

### Session activity

Two weekly counters on actions taken:

- **Sessions created**: how many snapshots you captured this week.
- **Tabs restored**: how many tabs were opened from a session.

Each one carries a small trend badge (up, down, stable) compared to the previous week. A **Lifetime** subtitle restates the historical totals for sessions created, restored, and archived.

### Categories and domains

- **Top session categories**: distribution of your sessions by category. A dash when no session has a category assigned.
- **Top domains**: a ranking of the domains that appear the most in your saved sessions.

### Sessions overview

Three complementary mini-views:

- **Timeline**: the oldest session and the most recently updated one, plus the number of sessions created this week.
- **Group color distribution**: a horizontal histogram aggregating the Chrome group colors across all sessions.
- **Largest sessions**: top sessions by tab count.
